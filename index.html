Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about switching to clean rooftop solar with SRH Solar.

We specialize in On-Grid (grid-tied with net-metering), Off-Grid (battery-backed for total independence), Hybrid Solar Systems, Solar Water Pumping for agriculture, and Solar Street Lighting.
Our Tier-1 solar panels have an operational lifespan of 25 to 30+ years, supported by standard 25-year linear performance warranties. Inverters typically carry 5 to 10-year manufacturer warranties.
The typical ROI payback period is 3 to 4 years for commercial/industrial setups and 4 to 5 years for residential rooftop systems. After this period, you generate free electricity for 20+ years.
On-grid systems draw power automatically and seamlessly from the utility grid at night. On cloudy days, modern Mono PERC panels still generate 20–40% power from diffused daylight.
