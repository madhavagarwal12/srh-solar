## 2. Core Service Offerings & Technical Details

### 1. On-Grid Solar System (Grid-Connected Photovoltaic System)
* **Description:** Works in direct synchrony with the local electrical power grid. Comprises high-efficiency solar modules, on-grid inverters, and bidirectional net-metering infrastructure.
* **Key Advantages:**
  * Ability to export surplus electricity back to the utility grid via Net Metering.
  * Significant reduction (up to 80-90%) in monthly electricity bills.
  * Clean, renewable power generation with the reliability of grid power backup when solar irradiance is low.
  * Eligible for central and state government rooftop solar subsidies (PM Surya Ghar / MNRE).

### 2. Off-Grid Solar Photovoltaic System
* **Description:** Autonomous, standalone solar system designed for areas without reliable electrical grid connections. Includes solar panels, battery bank (lithium / tubular), charge controller, and off-grid inverter.
* **Key Advantages:**
  * 100% energy independence and self-sufficiency.
  * Ideal for remote rural farms, farmhouse properties, factories, and off-grid establishments.
  * Zero reliance on grid outages, power cuts, or erratic voltage fluctuations.

### 3. Hybrid Solar System
* **Description:** Combines the best of both worlds: net-metered grid connection plus battery storage backup.
* **Key Advantages:**
  * Uninterrupted power supply during grid blackouts.
  * Smart energy management: battery charging during peak sun, powering home during peak grid tariff hours, export of excess energy.

### 4. Solar Power Water Pump
* **Description:** Designed for agricultural irrigation, rural drinking water schemes, livestock watering, and pond aeration. Integrates solar PV arrays with Bright Solar variable frequency drive (VFD) solar pump inverters and submersible/surface pumps.
* **Key Advantages:**
  * No diesel costs and zero grid electricity dependency.
  * Reliable, daytime pumping during high sunshine hours.
  * High durability, minimal operational expenditure, and long lifespan.

### 5. Solar-Powered Street Lights
* **Description:** Integrated and semi-integrated LED street lighting units powered by daytime solar charging with built-in battery storage, dusk-to-dawn sensors, and automatic dimming controllers.
* **Key Advantages:**
  * Zero trenching, cabling, or utility power required.
  * Ideal for rural roadways, gated townships, industrial campuses, and municipal pathways.
  * Low maintenance with weather-resistant housings.

### 6. The Smart Home Solution
* **Description:** Integrates rooftop solar generation with intelligent energy monitors, IoT home automation devices, and battery management systems.
* **Key Advantages:**
  * Real-time mobile app energy tracking and load management.
  * Optimized self-consumption reducing overall utility tariff brackets.

### 7. Solar Water Heaters & Supplies
* **Description:** Solar thermal systems for residential and commercial hot water requirements (evacuated tube and flat plate collectors).

### 8. Solar EPC & O&M (Operations & Maintenance)
* **Description:** End-to-end site inspection, shadow analysis, structure design, equipment procurement, testing & commissioning, net-metering approvals, and scheduled O&M cleaning/inspection services.

## 3. Site FAQs (Extracted Q&A)

1. **What types of solar panels are available?**
   * *Answer:* Common types include monocrystalline, polycrystalline, and thin-film panels. Monocrystalline panels are known for high efficiency and compact footprint, while polycrystalline panels offer cost-effective utility.
2. **How long do solar panels last?**
   * *Answer:* Most solar panels have a lifespan of 25 to 30 years or more, backed by 25-year linear power performance warranties.
3. **What factors affect the efficiency of solar panels?**
   * *Answer:* Ambient temperature, shading/obstructions, tilt angle & azimuth orientation, dust accumulation, and cell quality.
4. **What size solar panel system do I need for my home?**
   * *Answer:* System size depends on average monthly electricity consumption (kWh units), shadow-free roof area, and load requirements. A customized engineering assessment determines ideal capacity.
5. **How much will a solar panel system cost?**
   * *Answer:* Cost depends on system capacity (kW), panel type (DCR/Non-DCR Mono PERC/TOPCon), structure height, inverter brand, and battery backup requirements.
6. **Are there government incentives or tax credits?**
   * *Answer:* Yes, residential rooftop projects in India qualify for central government subsidies under the PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana, along with accelerated depreciation for commercial/industrial clients.
7. **How long does it take to recoup the investment (payback period)?**
   * *Answer:* The average ROI payback period ranges from 3 to 5 years for commercial setups and 4 to 6 years for residential, providing free electricity for the remaining 20+ years.
8. **Do solar panels require maintenance?**
   * *Answer:* Solar panels require very low maintenance—primarily periodic surface washing with clean water every 10–15 days to remove dust and debris.
9. **What happens on cloudy days or at night?**
   * *Answer:* On-grid systems draw power seamlessly from the utility grid at night. On cloudy days, panels still generate power from diffuse sunlight (20-40% capacity).
10. **What should I look for in a reputable solar installer?**
    * *Answer:* Certified EPC expertise, quality tier-1 equipment, strong engineering background, hassle-free net-metering liaisoning, and dependable post-installation O&M support.
11. **How do I monitor performance?**
    * *Answer:* Modern inverters include Wi-Fi/GPRS modules allowing daily, monthly, and real-time generation monitoring via smartphone apps.
